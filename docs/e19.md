## Conclusion

The maximum observed balanced-accuracy improvement from perfect prompt grouping is **+10.2620 pp** in the full-group variant. Developing an E19-B prompt-reconstruction prototype is justified: incomplete groups improved in all five seeds, by **3.53 to 4.17 pp** for groups capped at 10 responses and **4.29 to 8.16 pp** for groups capped at 18. These are still optimistic because the sampled rows retain their true group membership; E19-B must measure the cost of reconstructing groups incorrectly. The full-group result is an oracle ceiling.
