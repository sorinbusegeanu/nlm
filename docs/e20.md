## E20-D conclusion

E20-B versus E20-D mean fold BA standard deviation: 1.748 pp vs 0.552 pp.
On the E19-A incomplete-group samples, E20-D changed mean BA by -0.0385 pp for 2–10-row subsets and +0.3788 pp for 2–18-row subsets versus E20-B.
The stronger strategy is determined by OOF BA and its fold stability below; V0 remains development data and should not be treated as expected Kaggle score.
Across outer folds, E20-B mean BA was 77.8352% and E20-D mean BA was 78.2056%; E20-D won 4/5 folds. Recommend **E20-D** by aggregate OOF balanced accuracy, with fold variability considered.

## Findings

1. **Learned group features:** group features changed E20-B by -1.1496 pp versus the checkpoint-only + Hungarian ablation; they did not add value in this comparison.
2. **Hungarian assignment:** it added +1.1857 pp over E20-A and improved four of five outer folds; fold 5 regressed by 1.3091 pp.
3. **Reconstruction cost:** applying reconstructed rather than oracle groups to the same E13 utility scores lost 0.4084 pp BA (E19-A vs E19-B). E20-C is 0.9625 pp above E20-B, but that comparison also changes the classifier's group features and is not a pure grouping-only estimate.
4. **Kaggle readiness:** E20-B is not the preferred submission candidate yet: the group-feature ablation is weaker than checkpoint-only + Hungarian, fold 5 reverses the assignment benefit, and all V0 figures are development results. The checkpoint-only + reconstructed Hungarian variant deserves a separate deployment review.

## Model features and artifacts

Mean CatBoost importance is in `e20ab_feature_importance.csv`; separate checkpoint evidence and group feature rankings are in `e20ab_metrics.json`. `predictions_e20ab.parquet` and `scores_e20ab.npz` contain row-aligned OOF results. Fold-specific CatBoost models and nested grouping fits are under the E20 analysis directory.

E20-D outputs: `predictions_e20d.parquet`, `scores_e20d.npz`, `e20d_metrics.json`, `e20d_config.json`, `e20d_group_alignment.csv`, and `incomplete_group_predictions_e20d.parquet`. The five E20-C classifiers were hash-verified and loaded directly; no E20-D classifier was trained.

V0 is a development set and checkpoint selection has used V0; these scores are not expected Kaggle performance.

## Recommendation

E20-D is the stronger of these two training strategies on five-fold V0: it improved aggregate BA by 0.3701 pp and reduced fold-level BA SD from 1.748 to 0.552 pp. Incomplete-group simulations were mixed, so keep the Kaggle estimate provisional and compare on a separate holdout before deployment.
