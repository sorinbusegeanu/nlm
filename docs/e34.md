All figures are mean balanced accuracy across the same 50 500-row `v0_group` scenarios. These scenarios reuse V0 responses, so they are correlated robustness diagnostics, not independent validation.

E34-F is the only variant to improve on E32: +3.4065 to +3.8321 percentage points across the four decoders. It remains 0.2270 to 0.3690 points below E33-F. E34-C exactly reproduces E32 because no conditional exclusion rules qualified. A, D and E reduce accuracy; B is close to baseline but lower in each column.

E34 preserves the E32 60-checkpoint roster and frozen E32 router. E34-A/B/C use the E33 exclusion thresholds; E34-D/E apply E33's family-aggregated reliability factors; E34-F uses the E33-F CatBoost settings and 60-checkpoint candidate features. Rules and learned scores are fit with the saved five prompt-disjoint outer folds and four inner grouped folds for E34-F.

The group assignments for E19-F-A, E19-F-C and E19-D are the exact cached assignments used by E33. E32 baseline is recomputed from the frozen `scores_60` matrix and reproduces the archived E32 means exactly for all four decoders (absolute difference 0.0000).

E34-F uses 246 candidate features: six shared candidate/score features and four features for each of the 60 checkpoints (probability, rank, smoothed reliability ratio, and support). Its final deployable CatBoost classifier and full-training reliability statistics are recorded in `artifacts/analysis/e34/e34_deployment_manifest.json`. The comparable 40-checkpoint E33-F deployment classifier is saved in `artifacts/analysis/e33/e33_final_deployment.json`.

Artifacts: `artifacts/analysis/e34/metrics.json`, `oof_adjusted_scores.npz`, `scenario_predictions.parquet`, fold reliability statistics/counts, five fold classifiers, and the final five-fold-OOF deployment classifier. Timing methodology and measured rescoring costs are in [the E34 inference benchmark](e34_inference_benchmark.md).
