# E41 — E38 + E39 equal-probability blend

## Method

E41 is the arithmetic mean of the saved enhanced E38 SVC and enhanced E39 CatBoost 18-label probability matrices, with fixed weights 0.5/0.5. No models were retrained and no blend weights were tuned. Evaluation uses **V0 only** (2,699 rows), with canonical label order and V0 row IDs checked for alignment.

## V0 results

| Predictor | Accuracy | Balanced accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| E38 enhanced SVC | 48.20% | 48.19% |
| E39 enhanced CatBoost | 28.90% | 28.89% |
| E41 equal blend | **48.35%** | **48.34%** |

Against E38 alone, E41 changes 381 predictions: 92 errors are corrected and 88 correct predictions are lost, for a net gain of 4 correct predictions and +0.1486 percentage points balanced accuracy. Against E39, 697 errors are corrected and 172 correct predictions are lost.

The blend modestly improves on E38 alone on this V0 evaluation. It is a fixed exploratory blend result, not an independent generalization estimate. No V0-EXT data were loaded or scored for E41.

Artifacts: `artifacts/analysis/e41_e38_e39_blend/results.json` and `predictions.npz`.
