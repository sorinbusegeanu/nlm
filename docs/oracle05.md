## Fold-level performance and rule stability

Outer-fold metrics and rule IDs/support/thresholds are in `fold_metrics.csv`, `fold_rules.json`, and `rule_stability.csv`. ORACLE05 only applies an ordered top-1/top-2 swap; the pair itself is disjoint across rows, so at most one rule can fire. If multiple C evidence configurations qualify for the same ordered pair, the deterministic selection keeps one.

The strongest cross-fitted result is C_checkpoint (67.906% BA). The 95% prompt-group bootstrap intervals for improvement over ORACLE02 include zero for all three strategies, so the observed gains are suggestive rather than statistically clear.

## Answers

1. **Pairs with the strongest raw top-2 opportunity:** several low-support pairs tie at +2 net corrections; the most useful conditional candidates are gemini-3.8-flash → gemini-3.7-flash and claude-fable-5 → glm-5.3, where checkpoint evidence gates produce higher precision. Raw unconditional swaps are harmful for these pairs.
2. **Fixed pair rules:** strategy A reaches 67.167% cross-fitted BA versus ORACLE02 at 67.167%.
3. **Probability margins:** strategy B reaches 67.686%; its change relative to A is +0.519 pp.
4. **Checkpoint evidence:** strategy C reaches 67.906%; its change relative to B is +0.220 pp.
5. **Rules versus ORACLE04:** cross-fitted C uses 5.8 rules on average and reaches 67.906%; its optimistic six-rule full-V0 diagnostic reaches 69.614%. Neither approaches ORACLE04 combined at 71.169%.
6. **Balance of score and simplicity:** ranking the three tested strategy families gives C_checkpoint (67.906%, 5.8 rules), B_margin (67.686%, 1.4 rules), then A_pair (67.167%, no selected rules). C leads, but its bootstrap interval includes zero and its complexity/stability trade-off is not compelling enough to deploy.
7. **Kaggle:** No candidate is ready for submission: best ORACLE05 (C_checkpoint) is 67.906% BA, below ORACLE04 (71.169%); V0 also influenced checkpoint selection and prior rule development.

## Limitations and artifacts

The ORACLE02 training probabilities are nested prompt-held ORACLE02 predictions cached by ORACLE04. Checkpoint-based evidence uses full-V0 checkpoint probability caches and thus inherits their V0 checkpoint-selection exposure. Bootstrap intervals resample prompt groups and remain descriptive after V0-based model development.
