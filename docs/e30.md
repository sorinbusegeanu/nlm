## Inference implementation and packaging

The Kaggle notebook uses the packaged `src.e13_b_inference` functions, preserves test ID order, and reads checkpoint batch sizes from the measured per-checkpoint manifest with OOM downshift. The 17-checkpoint package is `zodrak/e30-checkpoints-v001`. Kernel version 6 completed with a passing runtime preflight and produced the independent, E19-F-A, and E19-F-C outputs. All three were submitted through the notebook-originated endpoint and are pending scoring: refs **58847908**, **58847913**, and **58847916**, respectively.

## Conclusion

- Nested prompt-group selection indicates small but positive average improvement for the greedy addition process; the cross-fitted step-2 estimate (the 17-member size) is only +0.148 pp over its 15-member fold-local baseline, so the improvement is modest and fold-sensitive.
- The fold-consensus final roster adds N0030 and N0006. On V0-derived scenarios it improves mean balanced accuracy in all 50 independent-decoder scenarios and improves in 46/50 E19-F-A and 45/50 E19-F-C scenarios.
- V0-derived scenario outcomes are a sensitivity analysis, not an independent test. External Kaggle scores must decide whether E30-B improves the deployed baseline.
