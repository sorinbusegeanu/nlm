The first candidate uses the source-independent rules in [`dataset_quality_criteria.md`](dataset_quality_criteria.md): explicit English language metadata, 50–2,000 response characters, valid response identity/label/provenance, explicit defect checks, normalized deduplication, and available validation isolation checks. It has zero normalized response overlap with V3.1. Its detailed audit and hashes are in [`glm53_flash_quality_en.md`](glm53_flash_quality_en.md).

The second candidate applies the same 50–2,000-character and isolation rules after deterministic English detection (seed 2026). It retained 9,716 of 9,719 rows; the only removals were three overlength responses. It has zero normalized-response overlap with V3.1. Row-level removals, source hashes, and label counts are recorded in its profile and audit. The source does not independently establish response licensing, so that provenance caveat is retained in the profile.

The third candidate is the downloadable 890-row Empero SFT snapshot. It retains 418 English responses at 50–2,000 characters, all labeled `gpt-5.6-luna`; 472 overlength rows were removed. It has zero exact normalized-response overlap with V3.1. Of the 418 retained rows, 384 match V1.3 and 34 are new relative to V1.3. Its card declares a non-commercial license, while the V1.3 copies are labeled CC-BY-4.0; this discrepancy is recorded for resolution before use.

The fourth candidate is the downloadable Best Distill GLM-5.3-Flash source. Its 18,112 rows yield 11,202 retained `glm-5.3-flash` responses after the standard quality filter. It has zero exact normalized-response overlap with V3.1 or the other queued V3.2 candidates. The Hugging Face license is marked `other`; no separate license terms are present in the repository, so resolve the license before use.

The fifth candidate is the 4,347-row canonical Kimi-K3 corpus, quality-filtered to 2,778 `kimi-k3` rows. Its language count uses `langdetect` on assistant text; code or structured answers may be false negatives, and the source license is heterogeneous and noncommercial. The source itself warns against assuming a downstream right; review the bundled license before use.

The sixth candidate is the 2,524-trace Saidutta Kimi-K3 Premium corpus, filtered to 965 `kimi-k3` rows. Its agent-traces and OpenAI-message views are byte-identical, so rows are counted once. Keep the per-source CC-BY-4.0/WTFPL/Apache-2.0 attribution stated in the source.

The seventh candidate is the Qwen-only subset of Saidutta’s Qwen/GLM/Kimi clean corpus: 23,922 `qwen3.8-max` rows. Its Kimi rows duplicate queue item 5 and are omitted from this candidate; `glm-5.2` is not a canonical NLM label and is excluded. The source license is marked other, with upstream/model-service restrictions documented in its profile.

The sixth candidate is Saidutta Kimi-K3 Premium. One 2,524-row corpus is represented by two byte-identical views; it contributes 965 retained `kimi-k3` rows. The source attributes data to CC-BY-4.0, WTFPL, and Apache-2.0 sources; preserve source-level attribution.
